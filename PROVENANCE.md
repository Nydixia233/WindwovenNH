# Source provenance

- WindwovenNH is an independent implementation. It does not reuse code, assets, motion traces, or build output from WaveyCapes, its legacy port, or other cape mods. There is no runtime dependency on a cape distribution service.
- The cloth solver uses the published position-based dynamics technique of Müller, Heidelberger, Hennix and Ratcliff (2006/2007), DOI `10.1016/j.jvcir.2007.01.005`. No implementation or media from the paper is included.
- Wrapper files and daemon toolchain metadata originate from `GTNewHorizons/ExampleMod1.7.10` at `bd8e4160b677466de3cfff2c4011a3806fa74942`; the original MIT notice is in `licenses/GTNH-ExampleMod-MIT.txt`. The Gradle wrapper remains Apache-2.0 (see `licenses/Gradle-Wrapper-Apache-2.0.txt`), and its properties pin the official distribution SHA-256. The build uses GTNHGradle 2.0.34. Forge and UniMixins (including Mixins and MixinExtras) are supplied separately. Nothing from the game, modpack, or shaders is bundled.
- The offline reference solver is an independently implemented double-precision simulator using published compliant position constraints (Macklin, Müller and Chentanez, *XPBD*, 2016), area-lumped mass and geometric drag. Fitting uses original synthetic scenarios. The head-contact proxy, wake recovery and fixtures are original changes. No third-party source, assets, reference meshes or motion captures are incorporated in the mod.
- The production mod renders cape textures that the installed game already selected. The QA fixture generates its own asymmetric atlas at runtime. The installed GregTech cape is referenced only for runtime visual testing, not copied into a release asset.
- Development and documentation were assisted by generative AI. WindwovenNH is not an official Minecraft, GTNH, or WaveyCapes release.
